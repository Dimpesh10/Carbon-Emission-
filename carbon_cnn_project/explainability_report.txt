=== Explainable AI using SHAP (Transformer Encoder) ===

1. Why needed:
Neural networks are typically 'Black Boxes'. SHAP allows us to see exactly which features drove the carbon emission predictions up or down.

2. How used:
We used shap.GradientExplainer on the trained Transformer Encoder, comparing the test samples against a background baseline of 100 training samples.

3. Results (Top 3 Drivers):
  - Industry Level
  - Vehicle Count
  - Humidity

4. Interpretation:
The model prediction is mainly influenced by Industry Level and Vehicle Count.

5. Dataset Comparison:
  - Average Dataset Emission (Scaled baseline): -0.3696
  - Current Sample Emission (Scaled): 0.7955
